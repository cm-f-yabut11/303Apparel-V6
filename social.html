Social media campaign

“Cop the Drop”: how 303 Apparel promotes its new collection on social media.

Campaign overview

Campaign

New product launch with a special discount: the “Cop the Drop” collection, with 15% off selected new arrivals using code 15FF until October 15, 2026.

Objective

Promote the new collection and send people to the 303 Apparel website to browse and order.

Audience

Teenagers aged 13 to 19 and young adults to adults aged 20 to 40 who are into fashion and personal style.

Platforms

Instagram and TikTok for visuals and short video, Facebook for announcements, X for quick updates and the last-call reminder.

Key message: Stay Ahead of the Trend. Call to action: Cop the Drop! Hashtags: #CopTheDrop #303Apparel #StayAheadOfTheTrend

Content schedule

A suggested four-phase plan that ends when the promo does.

  1. Sep 21 – 27
    Teaser
    Build curiosity

    Close-up shots of the collection with no prices. Countdown stickers to the drop.

  2. Sep 28 – Oct 4
    Drop
    Launch the collection

    Post each featured piece, announce code 15FF, and link to the products page.

  3. Oct 5 – 11
    Style challenge
    Get customers involved

    TikTok challenge: style one 303 piece casual and formal. Reshare the best entries.

  4. Oct 12 – 15
    Last call
    Create urgency

    Daily reminders that 15FF ends on October 15. Pin the offer on every profile.
